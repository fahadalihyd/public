StreakQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Summary

StreakQuest works without an account and has no backend server. Your challenges, streaks, XP, reflections and settings are stored on your device. The developer cannot see or access them. If you connect Google Drive, a copy of that data is saved in your own Google account.

Data stored on your device

Completed challenges, optional reflections, unlocked achievements, an in-progress timer, preferences, and custom challenges are stored on your device. This data stays on your device unless you enable Google Drive backup. It is deleted from the phone when you use “Clear local data” or uninstall the app.

Google Drive backup

Backup is optional. You can save a backup manually or on a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule. The backup is stored in a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive, which only this app can access using your Google sign-in. StreakQuest has no server and the developer cannot read that folder.

Uninstalling the app does not delete the backup. You can restore it after reinstalling with the same Google account. The backup is deleted through “Delete cloud backup” in Settings or by removing StreakQuest from Google Drive settings.

Reflections

Reflections are private notes. They stay in the local database and are included in a Google Drive backup only if you enable backup. They are not used for advertising.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in Settings or Android system settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

StreakQuest shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device’s advertising ID, IP address (used to infer approximate location), app interaction and ad performance data, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and, where consented, personalise ads.

Where required by law, including in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form is shown. You can change your choice from Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses data.

Children

StreakQuest is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use by children.

Health disclaimer

Challenges are general lifestyle activities, not medical, psychological or fitness advice. Adapt or skip any activity that does not feel right for you.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. For questions, contact the developer using the email address on the app’s Google Play listing.
